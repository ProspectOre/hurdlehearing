Why a Doctor of Audiology

Doctor of Audiology
A clinical doctorate, typically four years after a bachelor's degree, with a residency. Licensed to evaluate and diagnose hearing and balance disorders, refer for medical care, and treat.
Hearing instrument specialist
A separate state licence focused on selling and fitting hearing aids.

Questions to ask any hearing provider

  • What is your licence?
  • Will you diagnose, or only fit?
  • What happens if I need a physician?
  • What do follow-ups cost?
