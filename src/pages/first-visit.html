Your first visit

About an hour. Nothing is decided for you.

What happens

Four steps, in this order.

  1. A conversation about where hearing is hard for you. We ask about your health and what you want to hear again.
  2. A look in your ears. We check for wax or anything that needs a physician.
  3. The hearing test. Soft sounds at each pitch, speech in quiet and in noise, and how your middle ear responds.
  4. Results explained in plain language. You hear your options and decide in your own time.

What to bring

  • Photo ID and your insurance card.
  • Your current hearing aids and chargers.
  • A list of your medications.
  • A family member, if you like.
  • Your VA referral, if you are a veteran.

Cost

A hearing evaluation is billed to your insurance. We check your plan before the visit and tell you any cost.

CareCredit financing is available at both offices.

After the visit

You leave with your results in plain language and your options, if there are any.

You also get a written summary to take to your physician. (owner-confirm)

There is no pressure. Take the time you need to decide.

Three things that help

  • Notice where hearing is hardest: restaurants, television, the phone.
  • Bring someone whose voice you know well.
  • Write down your questions.
