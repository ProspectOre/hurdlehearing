Hearing care through your VA benefits

We are a VA Community Care provider. With a referral, your evaluation, hearing-aid fitting, and follow-up happen in Santa Maria or Arroyo Grande.

How a referral works

The Department of Veterans Affairs (VA) decides eligibility first.

  1. Ask your VA primary care provider for an audiology referral to community care.
  2. VA or TriWest, the company that runs Community Care in California, schedules your visit. You may also call us yourself.
  3. If you book yourself, email VeteranAppointments@TriWest.com within 24 hours with your referral number, and book within 21 days of TriWest's first contact.

What we do for veterans

  • Hearing evaluations
  • Fitting and follow-up of VA-issued hearing aids
  • Repairs and cleaning
  • Tinnitus management
  • Compensation and Pension hearing exams

Exact services under your referral are confirmed by VA. (owner-confirm wording)

What to bring

  • Your referral or authorization number
  • Your VA ID
  • Your current hearing aids

Cost

Under an approved referral there is typically no cost to you. VA pays the community provider directly. (owner-confirm)

Already a VA patient?

The Santa Maria VA Clinic offers audiology in person and online. It is on the same block as our Santa Maria office.

Community care is one of two routes. VA staff will help you choose.
