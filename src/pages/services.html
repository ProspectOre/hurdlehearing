Services

Everything for your hearing, in two offices, under a Doctor of Audiology.

Hearing evaluations

A full diagnostic test, read by a Doctor of Audiology.

  • A look in your ears.
  • Soft sounds at each pitch.
  • Speech in quiet and in noise.
  • How your middle ear responds. Results explained the same day.

Hearing aids

Fitted to your hearing and your life, then adjusted until right.

We fit at the first visit and adjust at follow-ups, until the aids sound right to you.

Brands include Signia, Oticon, Unitron, and Widex. (owner-confirm which are stocked)

Veterans: we fit and service VA-issued hearing aids. How VA referrals work

Repairs, cleaning, batteries

Walk in during office hours, any brand, wherever you bought it.

Cleaning, batteries, and minor repairs, often the same day. (owner-confirm)

Larger repairs go to the maker and we tell you how long.

Office hours and directions

Tinnitus

Evaluation and management for ringing in the ears.

  • An evaluation to find what is behind the ringing.
  • Counselling and sound therapy.
  • Hearing aids that can quiet tinnitus for many people.

Earwax removal

Safe removal in the office.

When wax blocks your hearing or a hearing aid, we remove it here. No referral needed.

Custom earmolds and protection

Made for your ears.

  • Swim plugs.
  • Musician plugs.
  • Protection for work and the range.

Assistive listening devices

For where hearing aids are not enough.

  • Phones that show what the caller says.
  • Television listeners.
  • Devices for meetings, church, and large rooms.
