Accessibility

This site is built for people who are hard of hearing, have low vision, or are new to the web.

Our standard

We aim for WCAG 2.2 AA, and go beyond it for text contrast and size.

Every page is tested with a keyboard, screen readers, 200% zoom, and color-blindness simulation.

We do not use accessibility overlay widgets.

On this site

  • Text is at least 18px, with 7:1 contrast.
  • Every page works by keyboard.
  • Links are underlined.
  • No motion, autoplay, or sound.
  • Captions and transcripts on any video.
  • Forms with plain error messages.
  • Phone numbers you can tap.

At the offices

  • Wheelchair access.
  • Quiet waiting area.
  • Staff communicate in writing.
  • Relay and captioned phone calls welcome.
